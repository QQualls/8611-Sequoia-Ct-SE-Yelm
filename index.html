About This Home

Freshly painted 3 bedroom, 2 bath home on a 0.31-acre lot in Nisqually Pines. Vaulted ceilings and skylights fill the open kitchen with natural light. It has a large island, plenty of cabinet space, a built-in desk nook and a tall pantry cabinet. Hard surface flooring runs through the living, dining and bedroom areas. The primary bedroom has its own bath with a walk-in shower and a long vanity, and the hall bath has a tub/shower. There’s a separate utility room at the back entry, plus central A/C. Outside you’ll find a long gravel driveway with plenty of parking, a deck at the back door, mature trees, and several outbuildings for storage, tools or hobbies. Nisqually Pines residents get community amenities including private Nisqually River access, parks and a seasonal pool. Minutes to downtown Yelm with easy access to JBLM. Vacant and easy to show.
